## project Overview 

KICKS is a modern sneaker brand established in 2026 with the aim of providing fashionable, comfortable, and affordable sneakers for people of all ages. The brand focuses on combining quality craftsmanship with the latest fashion trends. Since its launch, KICKS has expanded its product range to include casual, sports, and limited-edition sneakers.
